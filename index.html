Section 02

Logo & Symbol

A geometric chevron from three planes: Royal Authority Blue, Precision Red, and Structural Silver. KneuraLens™ must appear on first use in every document.

✓ DO
✓ Use on white, Canvas White, or Deep Navy backgrounds only
✓ Maintain clear space equal to the mark's full height on all 4 sides
✓ Use the full-colour version as the default in all communications
✓ Use the white version on Dark Navy or solid blue backgrounds
✓ Include alt text: 'Kneuralabs logo' in all digital uses
✗ DO NOT
✗ Place on photographic backgrounds without a colour block behind it
✗ Scale below 48px (digital) or 15mm (print)
✗ Recolour any element of the mark independently
✗ Rotate, skew, or apply drop shadows to the mark
✗ Use the mark as a decorative pattern or texture tile

Section 08

Digital & Motion

Animation is functional. It confirms state changes, guides attention, and signals transitions. Every motion decision must pass: if removed, does the user lose information?

Interactive Element States

All interactive elements transition at 150ms (micro) or 300ms (component). Hover states use Deep Navy fill with a 2px lift shadow.

Duration Scale

Three tiers of timing. Micro for feedback, component for reveals, page for transitions. Stagger list items at 50ms intervals.

Section 09

Regulatory Standards

Three frameworks. One foundation. Incorrect citation damages credibility — cite with precision, always.

NIST AI RMF
NIST AI Risk Management Framework
U.S. federal voluntary standard; de facto audit and procurement benchmark for all sectors. Growing commercial requirement in enterprise RFPs and federal contracting.
Voluntary · U.S. Standard
ISO/IEC 42001:2023
ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI Management System
International certification standard for AI management systems; audited by accredited third parties. Increasingly written into vendor qualification requirements globally.
International Certification
EU AI Act
Regulation (EU) 2024/1689
Applies to any organisation deploying AI to EU-based users, regardless of incorporation country. Fines up to 3% of global annual turnover for non-compliance.
Mandatory · EU Regulation
Citation Rules
→First use: spell out the full name followed by the abbreviation in parentheses
→ISO/IEC 42001 always includes the year: 42001:2023 — never '42001' alone
→NIST AI RMF: always a space between NIST and AI — not 'NISTAI RMF'
→Never describe NIST AI RMF as 'mandatory' — it is voluntary; commercial pressure is distinct from legal obligation
